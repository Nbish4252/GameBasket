Copper & Ash
Marrow Studios · PC
Played with

Went in expecting a straightforward western revenge plot and came out thinking about it for a week. The pacing in the second act drags a little around the ranch side-quests, but the final confrontation earns every hour leading up to it.

Combat is simple but the writing carries this one. Ash's dialogue options actually change how NPCs treat you three towns later, which I wasn't expecting from a studio this small.
